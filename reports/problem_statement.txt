Project: ShopSense, Customer Shopping Behavior Analytics

Business problem: A retail company wants to understand how its customers shop so it can increase sales, improve customer satisfaction, and build long-term loyalty. Today, the business lacks a clear view of who its customers are, what they buy, and how they respond to discounts, promo codes, and subscriptions.

Objective: Analyze customer purchase data to uncover shopping patterns across demographics (age, gender, location), product categories, and customer segments, and turn those findings into actionable recommendations for marketing and sales teams.

Key questions:

Which age groups and genders generate the most revenue?
Which product categories and items sell best?
Do discounts and promo codes influence spending?
How do new, returning, and loyal customers differ?
Does subscription status affect purchase behavior?

Deliverables: A cleaned dataset, SQL analysis, an interactive Power BI dashboard, a project report, and a presentation.
